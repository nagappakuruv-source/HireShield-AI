OUR PURPOSE

Why HireShield exists

Finding a job should be an opportunity, not a risk. Suspicious postings can mislead applicants through false promises, payment requests and misleading claims. HireShield aims to make these warning signs easier to recognize.

THE TECHNOLOGY

How the system works

HireShield combines multiple analysis techniques to provide a more informative assessment.

01

Machine Learning

A TF-IDF and Logistic Regression model examines job posting text and predicts whether it resembles genuine or fraudulent examples in its training data.

02

Risk Assessment

A rule-based engine checks warning signs such as registration fees, guaranteed placements, urgency tactics and suspicious claims.

03

AI Explanation

An AI-generated explanation helps users understand detected warning signs and practical precautions.

IMPORTANT TO KNOW

Use analysis as guidance, not a guarantee.

HireShield provides automated predictions and risk indicators. A low-risk result does not guarantee that a job or company is legitimate. Always verify the employer through independent sources and never pay money to secure a job.
